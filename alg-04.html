Level 1: Rapid Multiplication
What is Substitution?

Substitution means replacing a variable (like x) with a given number. Think of x as an empty Drop-In Box waiting for a number!

Drop in x = 4
3 x + 2 ➔ 3 × 4 + 2
12 + 2 = 14

Remember the Invisible Multiplication rule: 3x means 3 × x. When x = 4 drops in, calculate 3 × 4 = 12 first!

The Concatenation Trap!

Never glue the numbers together side-by-side when substituting!

If x = 4, what is 2x?
2x
≠
24
2x = 2 × (4) = 8

In arithmetic, putting 2 and 4 next to each other makes 24. In Algebra, 2x always means 2 multiplied by x!

BODMAS Order of Operations

After dropping the number into the box, always Multiply BEFORE you Add or Subtract:

Find 2x + 5 when x = 3:
Step 1 (Multiply):   2 × (3) = 6
Step 2 (Add 5):   6 + 5 = 11
⚠️ BODMAS Trap: Do NOT add 3 + 5 = 8 first and then multiply by 2!

Tap the Drop-In Box to replace x, then solve Step 1 and Step 2.
