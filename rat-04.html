The Problem with Different Sizes

If you have a half and a third, you cannot just say you have "two" of something, because the slices are different sizes.

Slices are mismatched! You cannot add them together yet.

To fix this, we must physically slice both pies into smaller, matching pieces. This is called finding the Lowest Common Denominator.

Scaling the Fractions
