⚠️ Unequal Parts!

You cannot add a half-pizza to a quarter-pizza and call it "two pizzas". You must make the pieces equal first.

Step 1: Find the LCM

Find the Lowest Common Multiple (LCM). Count in your multiples for both numbers until you find the first match.

Multiples of 3: 3, 6, 9, 12, 15...

Multiples of 4: 4, 8, 12, 16...

The LCM is 12. This becomes your new Common Denominator.

Step 2: Make Equivalent Fractions

Look at the first fraction (1/3). Ask yourself: "How does a 3 become a 12?"

We multiply it by 4. Because the bottom multiplied by 4, the top must also multiply by 4.
