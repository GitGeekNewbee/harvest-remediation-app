Fluency Gatekeeper

Before you shift commas, you must prove you can rapidly multiply and divide whole numbers by powers of 10.

The 3-Level Pathway:
  • Level 1: Multiply by 10, 100, 1000
  • Level 2: Divide by 10, 100, 1000
  • Level 3: Mixed Operations

Target: 15 questions per level at 90% accuracy.

Level 1: Multiply by 10s

Multiplication (Bigger)

When you multiply by 10, the number must get bigger. The comma jumps to the RIGHT.

One zero in 10 means ONE jump to the right.

The number of zeros in 10, 100, or 1000 tells you exactly how many spaces the comma needs to jump.
