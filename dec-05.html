Level 1: Times Tables

The Golden Rule

Never line up the commas when multiplying decimals. It creates massive confusion.

The Jump Counting Method:

  1. Ignore the commas and multiply as whole numbers.
  2. Count the total decimal places in the question.
  3. Jump the comma back into the answer.

By converting them to whole numbers first, you eliminate the risk of messy vertical decimal addition.
