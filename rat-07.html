How many 1/6 slices can you fit perfectly into 1/2 of a pie?

Because division counts the number of pieces that fit, dividing by a fraction often gives you a larger whole number as an answer.

The Keep-Change-Flip Rule

Drawing pies takes too long. Mathematics uses a shortcut algorithm called Keep-Change-Flip (KCF).

Keep the first fraction exactly the same.
Change the division sign to multiplication.
Flip the second fraction upside down (its reciprocal).
