The Division Illusion

Dividing fractions looks terrifying, but it is actually a trick.

You never actually divide fractions. We use a method called K.C.F.

K - KEEP the first fraction exactly the same.
C - CHANGE the division sign to multiplication.
F - FLIP the second fraction upside down.
