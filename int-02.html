Why Subtraction Tricks Us

Subtracting a negative number looks confusing because the minus sign is doing two different jobs at once.

DANGER: Double Minus Signs

−
(-3)
Taking away a negative debt actually moves you UP into the positives!

Instead of guessing in your head, we rewrite every subtraction problem as an addition problem first.

Keep – Change – Change

To safely turn subtraction into addition, follow three steps: Keep – Change – Change.

1. KEEP +4
2. CHANGE +
3. CHANGE +3
(+4) − (-3) ➔ (+4) + (+3) = 7

Keep the first number as it is. Change the minus sign to plus. Change the second number to its opposite.

The "Two Negatives" Trap

Be careful! Adding two negative numbers does NOT make a positive answer.

EXAMPLE: (-3) − (+5)
(-3) − (+5) ➔ (-3) + (-5)
3 Red (-1) Tiles + 5 Red (-1) Tiles
+8 ✓ -8

When both groups are negative, there are no Zero Pairs to cancel out. All 8 tiles are negative, so the final answer is -8.
