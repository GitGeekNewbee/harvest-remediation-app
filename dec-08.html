The Language of Math

Word problems hide the math operations inside everyday language. Look for the keywords:

➕ Add: "Total", "Altogether", "Sum"
➖ Subtract: "Change", "Difference", "Left over"
✖️ Multiply: "Each", "Per", "Total cost of many"
➗ Divide: "Split", "Shared equally", "Portions"

Read the sentence carefully to decide which operation you need before you touch the numbers.

The Currency Trap

In Financial Mathematics, whole numbers are traps. They hide invisible commas and zeros.

Thabo buys a pie for R15,50. He pays with a R50 note. How much change does he receive?

Wrong Setup:

50 - 15,50 = 34,50 (Forgot to borrow!)

Correct Setup:

50,00 - 15,50 = 34,50

Always add ,00 to whole Rands before you subtract!
