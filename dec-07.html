Level 1: Division
The Float Strategy

When you divide a decimal by a whole number, there is only one rule you must remember: Float the Comma.

3,2
4
12,8

The decimal comma moves straight up from the house into the answer. It never shifts left or right.

Divide Normally

Once the comma is floated up, you just pretend the numbers are whole and divide normally.

3
,
2
4
12,8
12 ÷ 4 = 3
8 ÷ 4 = 2

Ignore the comma while doing the math. Just make sure your numbers line up above the house.

Placeholder Zeros

Sometimes a number is too small to divide into. You MUST place a zero on top to hold the spot!

0
,
0
5
3
0,15

3 cannot go into 0, so we write 0. 3 cannot go into 1, so we write 0. 3 goes into 15 five times.
