Level 1: Division
The Float Strategy

When you divide a decimal by a whole number, there is only one rule you must remember: Float the Comma.

3,2
4
12,8

The decimal comma moves straight up from the house into the Final Answer. It never shifts left or right.

Divide Normally

Once the comma is floated up, you just divide the digits as whole numbers.

Divide step-by-step and make sure your digits line up above the house.

Placeholder Zeros

Sometimes a digit is too small to divide into. You MUST write a zero on top to hold its place!
