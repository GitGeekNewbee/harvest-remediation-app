The Language of Fractions

Before calculating, identify what each number does.

Numerator
Denominator

Numerator: The top number. It counts how many pieces you actually have.

Denominator: The bottom number. It tells you the total size of the pieces (like how the pizza was cut).

Step 1: Adding Common Parts

When denominators match, parts are the same size. Keep the bottom denominator unchanged. Add the top numbers.
