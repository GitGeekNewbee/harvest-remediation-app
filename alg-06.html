Level 1: Sum & Difference
The 4 Core Maths Action Words

In Maths, specific English words tell you which operation symbol to write in an algebraic expression:

Sum / More than+
Difference / Less than−
Product of×
Quotient of÷
"The sum of 3x and 4" ➔ 3x + 4

Remember that in Algebra, "the product of 3 and x" means 3 × x, which we write neatly side-by-side as 3x.

Product & Quotient in Expressions

When a sentence combines product or quotient with addition or subtraction, translate the multiplication or division part first:

EXAMPLE 1 (PRODUCT & SUM):
"The sum of the product of 4 and x, and 7"
➔ 4x + 7
EXAMPLE 2 (QUOTIENT & DIFFERENCE):
"The difference between the quotient of 6x and 2, and 5"
➔ (6x ÷ 2) − 5 = 3x − 5

Also remember the Invisible 1 Rule: if a sentence just says x, its coefficient is 1x!

The "Less Than" Reversal Trap!

Watch out for the phrases "less than" and "subtracted from" — they reverse the word order!

Translate: "5 less than x"
❌ Trap (Word-for-Word Order): Writing 5 − x because 5 comes first in the sentence!
✓ Correct Translation: "5 less than x" means start with x (1x) and take 5 away: x − 5 (written as 1x − 5).
