The Sign Rule for Dividing

Division follows the exact same sign rule as multiplication:

SAME SIGNS
(+) ÷ (+) = +
(-) ÷ (-) = +
Always POSITIVE
DIFFERENT SIGNS
(+) ÷ (-) = −
(-) ÷ (+) = −
Always NEGATIVE

If the two signs match, the answer is positive. If the two signs are different, the answer is negative.

Avoid the Two Division Traps

Never apply addition rules when dividing integers!

TRAP 1: TWO NEGATIVES DIVIDING
(-20) ÷ (-4) = -5 ✓ +5
TRAP 2: BIGGER POSITIVE DIVIDEND
(+20) ÷ (-4) = +5 ✓ -5
