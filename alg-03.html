The Inverse Operation Sign Trap

Always flip the sign when cancelling a number across the equals sign!

• Opposite of + 4 is − 4   (x + 4 = 10 ➔ x = 10 − 4 = 6)
• Opposite of − 4 is + 4   (x − 4 = 10 ➔ x = 10 + 4 = 14)
• Opposite of × 3 is ÷ 3   (3x = 12 ➔ x = 12 ÷ 3 = 4)

If you keep the same sign instead of flipping to the opposite operation, the scale tips over!

Two-Step Equations

For an equation like 2x + 3 = 11, remove the constant (+ 3) first, then divide by the coefficient (2):

Step 1 (Subtract 3):   2x = 11 − 3 = 8
Step 2 (Divide by 2):   x = 8 ÷ 2 = 4
⚠️ Never combine 2x + 3 into 5x! Remove + 3 from both sides first.

Use the Balance Scale to choose the correct Inverse Operation before solving.
