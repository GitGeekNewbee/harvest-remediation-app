What is a Fraction?

A fraction is just a way to show a piece of something. Think of a pizza.

Numerator (Top): 1
How many slices you have (the colored slice).

Denominator (Bottom): 4
The TOTAL number of equal slices that make up the whole pizza.

The Golden Rule: Equal Parts

Fractions only work if every single piece is exactly the same size.
