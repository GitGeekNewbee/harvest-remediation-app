Level 1: Number Bonds

What is a Fraction?

A fraction is a way to describe equal parts of a whole.

If you have a pizza cut into 4 slices, and 1 slice is shaded, you have 1 out of 4 parts.

The Denominator

The bottom number is the Denominator. It tells you the total size of the pieces (how many pieces make a full whole).

?
4

In our pizza, the whole is cut into 4 pieces, so 4 goes on the bottom.

The Numerator

The top number is the Numerator. It counts exactly how many of those pieces you actually have (the shaded parts).

1
4

There is 1 shaded slice, so 1 goes on top.

The Flipped Trap!

A massive mistake is putting the total number of slices on top and the shaded slices on the bottom.

4
1

Do not flip them! The total always lives on the bottom.
