What is a Percentage?

"Per cent" means out of 100. Look at the % sign — it has one line and two zeros, just like 100!

35 coloured squares out of 100 equal squares is 35%, or 0,35 as a decimal.

Columns of 10% and Squares of 1%

Every full vertical column has 10 squares (10%). Every single square is 1%.

6 full columns = 60%
4 single squares = 4%
Total = 64% (0,64)

Counting by 10% columns first makes shading and reading a 100-grid fast and accurate.
