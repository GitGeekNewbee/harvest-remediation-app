Step 2: Count Total Decimal Places

3 Total Places

There is 1 digit behind the comma in 0,3, and 2 digits behind the comma in 0,04. That equals 3 Total Places.

Step 3: Place the Comma

Place the decimal comma in your Final Answer so that it has the exact same number of decimal places.

Whole Number Product: 12

0,012

We need 3 decimal places, and 12 only has 2 digits, so we put a placeholder zero in front to get the Final Answer 0,012.
