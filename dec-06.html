Level 1: Division

The Whole Number Puzzle

We can make dividing decimals easy by turning it into a whole number puzzle.

The Balance Rule:

You are not allowed to change just one number in a division problem. Whatever jumps you give the second number, you must also give the first number to keep them perfectly balanced.

Our goal is to make the second number a whole number so it is easy to work with.

Watch The Balance

Let's balance this equation: 0,125 ÷ 0,25

The second number is 0,25. We need to jump its comma to the right until it is a whole number.

Ignore and Restore

Now we just solve the new balanced equation: 12,5 ÷ 25

Step 1: Ignore the comma.
Just divide the basic numbers: 125 ÷ 25 = 5
Step 2: Put it back.
Look at the first number (12,5). It has 1 decimal place. Give your answer 1 decimal place.

Final Answer: 0,5
