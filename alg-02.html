Adding vs. Multiplying Coefficients

When combining like terms connected by +, you ADD the front numbers (coefficients) and keep the letter the same:

3x + 4x = 7x
⚠️ Watch out:
• Do NOT multiply 3 × 4 = 12x when adding!
• Remember: x + x = 2x (whereas x × x = x²)
