Fluency Gatekeeper

Decimal fractions are just fractions with a denominator of 10 or 100. Pass 3 rapid levels to prove you can convert them instantly.

The 3-Level Pathway:
  • Level 1: Tenths (e.g., 3/10 = 0,3)
  • Level 2: Double-Digit Hundredths
  • Level 3: Single-Digit Hundredths (e.g., 4/100)

Note: In South Africa, always use a comma ( , ) for decimals!

Level 1: Tenths

What is a Decimal?

A decimal is just a quick way to write a fraction out of 10, 100, or 1000 without drawing the fraction line.

The Whole-Number Illusion

Which number is larger? 0,2 or 0,15?

Because 15 is a bigger number than 2, many people guess 0,15. Let's look at the blocks.

0,2
2 tenths
vs
0,15
15 hundredths

0,2 fills two entire columns (20 blocks). 0,15 only fills 15 blocks. 0,2 is larger!

The Invisible Zero Rule

To avoid the illusion, you can always add an invisible zero to the end of a decimal without changing its value.

0,2
=
0,20

If you compare 0,20 and 0,15, it instantly makes sense. 20 blocks is more than 15 blocks.
