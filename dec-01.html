Tenths

Fractions and Decimals are two different ways to write the exact same part of a whole.

Fraction:
3
10
Decimal:
0,3

The first place behind the decimal comma is the Tenths column. 3 out of 10 strips is written as 0,3.

Hundredths

If we cut the whole block into 100 equal squares, we use the second place behind the comma.

Fraction:
45
100
Decimal:
0,45

We have 4 full columns (4 tenths) and 5 small squares (5 hundredths). The Final Answer is 0,45.

The Placeholder Zero Trap

Watch out! Do not mix up Tenths and Hundredths.

3 Hundredths is only 3 tiny squares (0,03). You MUST write a zero in the tenths place to hold the spot!
